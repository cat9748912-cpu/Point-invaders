Privacy Policy

Who is responsible

Point Invaders is run by ⟦BUSINESS NAME⟧, Singapore. For anything about your data, including requests under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act, contact our data protection contact at cat9748912@gmail.com.

The short version. Your username, scores and what you have unlocked are public: they are what the leaderboards are made of. We never see your card details. We do not sell your data or show you ads.

What we keep, and why

Your profile

  • A random player ID for every profile, from Firebase Authentication.
  • For a saved account: your email address and how you sign in. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Firebase Authentication; the game itself uses only the email address.
  • A guest profile is stored in your browser and on the online boards only while you are connected. It is deleted when you press Exit or clear your browser’s site data.

What other players can see

  • Public: your username and your game record: points, credits, level, scores and personal bests, medals, achievements and title, streaks, cosmetics owned and worn, rivals, crew, bounties, challenges and the daily and weekly boards. Anyone can read these; that is how the boards and player cards work. Please do not use your real name as a username.
  • Supporters: if you buy the Supporter Pass, a public ◆ SUPPORTER mark and the date you became a supporter are shown with your profile.
  • Signed-in players: chat messages you send in the grid terminal, with your username, and multiplayer rooms you join.
  • Public: Brick Lab levels you publish, with your author name.

Purchases

  • Which items your account owns and, for each order: the item, amount, currency, date, status and the payment provider’s reference numbers.
  • Your card details, billing address and the email for your receipt are collected by Stripe and Link on the checkout page, not by us. Link is the merchant of record and handles the payment under its own privacy policy.

Feedback

If you send feedback from ⚙️ Settings, we store your message, rating and optional handle, with your player ID, guest status, points, credits, difficulty setting, the time, your browser’s user agent and your screen size, to help us reproduce problems. It is also emailed to us through Formspree.

Anonymous statistics

The game counts rounds started, finished and quit, time played, and whether 3D or a touch screen was used, per mission and per day. These counters contain no player ID or name.

On your device

The game saves a copy of your profile and your settings in your browser’s storage, so it opens quickly and works offline. This includes your loadouts, practice ghosts, tutorial progress and which items you own. It stays on your device. Clearing site data removes it.

Who else is involved

  • Google Firebase: sign-in (Authentication), the game database (Realtime Database, hosted in Singapore) and the purchase server (Cloud Functions, Singapore). Firebase also processes technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service.
  • Stripe and Link: checkout and payment, as merchant of record.
  • Formspree: delivers feedback to our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and keeps ordinary server logs.
  • Google Fonts and Google’s content network: your browser downloads the fonts and the Firebase code from Google’s servers, which see your IP address.

Some of these providers process data outside Singapore, including in the United States, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

Our reasons

We use your data to run the game and your account, to deliver what you buy, to keep the boards fair, to fix problems, and to understand which missions people play. For players in the EU or the UK, the legal bases are: performing our contract with you (the game, your account and purchases), our legitimate interests (security, fair play, fixing bugs, anonymous statistics), and legal obligations (keeping records of purchases).

How long we keep it

  • Guest profiles: until you exit or clear your browser data.
  • Saved accounts: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Purchase records: as long as the account exists, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Feedback and chat: until we no longer need them to run the arcade.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account, by writing to cat9748912@gmail.com. Deleting a saved account deletes its progress and the items bought on it. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority, which in Singapore is the Personal Data Protection Commission.

Children

Point Invaders is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Players aged 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian’s permission to buy anything.

Security

All traffic is encrypted. The database only lets each player change their own record, and only our server can record what a player has bought. Card details never pass through our pages.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and tell you in the game about important changes.
