🎛️ RENDER MODE

Flip the whole arcade between flat neon and real 3D — instantly, no sign-out. Your next mission renders in the new mode; points and leaderboards are shared.

✨ GRAPHICS

How much detail the 3D renderer draws. Ultra Realistic is the sharpest image and the heaviest on the GPU; Normal keeps the frame rate smooth. Stored per device, and it applies in 3D mode.

🎮 INPUT

Haptics buzz on hits, absorbs, scores and fails. With the gamepad on, the left stick aims, A acts, B closes, and START quits.
